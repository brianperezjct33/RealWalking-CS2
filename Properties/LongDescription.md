Walk through your Cities: Skylines II city with ground-aware first-person navigation. Walk along real pedestrian paths, use stairs and station areas, and board or leave supported transit vehicles.

Controls: Alt+F to enter or leave; WASD move on foot; Shift run; mouse look; E to board or disembark at a valid stop; Ctrl+Shift+J switches navigation/free flight (and releases the vehicle into flight while riding); Ctrl+Shift+V toggles all developer visuals; Esc exits first person. WASD does not disembark. Boarding requires the vehicle to be fully stopped (including at red lights or in traffic); moving vehicles cannot be boarded. Bus/tram disembarking also requires a sustained stop and a safe landing. Free flight holds absolute world height independent of terrain and water; R/F changes flight height. Boarding is disabled in free flight.

Standalone edition: includes a modified lightweight First Person Camera Continued engine. No separate FPC mod is needed. Do NOT enable this alongside First Person Camera Continued, earlier FPC forks, or the future Real Walking FPC Edition. Disable other camera mods and restart your game.

Features: ground-following walking, pedestrian network and Navigation Area surfaces, stairs, E-key public transit boarding, route/stop information, and clean Alt+F view with restored UI on exit. Transit exit depends on validated nearby walkable geometry; it may be unavailable at some custom stations.
