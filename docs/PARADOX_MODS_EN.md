# Real Walking — Standalone

## Short description
Explore your city on foot, use stairs and pedestrian paths, and ride public transport in first person. Camera included.

## Full description
**Explore the city you built — at street level.**

Real Walking brings pedestrian-aware first-person exploration to **Cities: Skylines II**. Press **Alt+F** to enter near the camera's nearest safe walkable surface, then roam streets, pedestrian areas, stairs, station platforms and waterfronts. The mod combines a navigation-aware walking system with an embedded first-person camera and supported public-transport riding.

### Features
- **Walkable-world navigation:** follow pedestrian networks and Navigation Areas, including stairs, bridges and station passages, with surface-aware camera height.
- **Easy entry:** Alt+F finds a nearby valid walking surface based on **3D distance from the camera**, rather than requiring a precisely aimed screen-center ray.
- **Navigation and Free flight:** switch between guided walking and unrestricted, absolute-height free flight. Terrain and waves do not automatically change flight altitude.
- **Public transport:** use **E** to board supported stopped buses, trams, metro trains, passenger trains, ferries and passenger ships. Ride with the vehicle, then disembark where the mod can verify a safe landing. Moving vehicles cannot be boarded; moving buses/trams cannot be exited with E.
- **Routes and stops:** view line and stop information while riding, including long station names.
- **Optional diagnostics:** one shortcut to toggle pedestrian paths, Navigation Areas, estimated walking widths and radar overlays.
- **Localized interface:** 12 Real Walking UI locales.

### Controls
| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Alt+F** | Enter / exit first person |
| **WASD** / **Mouse** | Move / look |
| **Shift** | Move faster while walking |
| **E** | Board a stopped supported vehicle / disembark when safe |
| **Ctrl+Shift+J** | Navigation ↔ Free flight; detach from a ride into Free flight |
| **R / F** | Ascend / descend in Free flight |
| **Ctrl+Shift+V** | Toggle all navigation debug overlays |
| **Esc** | Exit first person |

### Important compatibility information
**This is the Standalone edition.** It includes an adapted **First Person Camera Continued** engine. **Do not enable a separate FPC Continued mod, older FPC fork, or another Real Walking camera edition in the same playset.** Disable conflicting camera mods and restart the game before testing.

Tested with **Cities: Skylines II 1.6.x**. Navigation is based on the walkable geometry the game exposes; some custom stations or unusual waterfronts may not provide a safe entry or exit. This mod does not intentionally change the underlying traffic AI, pedestrian simulation or saved city entities. Save corruption risk is expected to be low; back up important cities.
